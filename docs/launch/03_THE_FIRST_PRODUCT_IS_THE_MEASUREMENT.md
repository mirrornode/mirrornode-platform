# Draft 03 — The First Product Is the Measurement

**Status:** DRAFT / NOT PUBLISHED

If a company says it needs a better AI control layer, the first question should not be “which platform should you buy?”

It should be:

> What is your current system actually dependent on, who can do what inside it, and what evidence survives when something changes?

That is why MIRRORNODE's first paid product is not the full control infrastructure.

It is the measurement.

## Osiris Audit v1

Osiris Audit v1 is a one-pass, human-reviewed structural assessment of a live AI system across three dimensions:

### Control

Where consequential authority actually lives.

Does a model merely propose, or can it mutate? Is approval external and attributable? Are deployment, publishing, tool use, and execution distinct? Can an actor enlarge its own permissions?

### Continuity

Which required functions are coupled to one provider, token, model, context window, credential, or machine.

Is the function specified independently enough to move? Is replacement eligibility tested or assumed? Is there an owned path for the functions that cannot be allowed to disappear?

### Evidence

What remains after the session ends.

Can the customer reconstruct what was reviewed, what target was involved, what was authorized, what changed, and what remains unknown? Or is the operating history trapped inside chat logs and human memory?

## What the customer receives

The current pilot deliverable is intentionally bounded:

- System & Dependency Map;
- Substrate Exposure Findings;
- Authority Boundary Findings;
- Evidence Continuity Gaps;
- Prioritized Remediation Path;
- explicit Unknowns & Scope Limits.

The audit reviews up to five primary authorized artifacts or links and includes one clarification pass.

The pilot price is $149.

## Why there is no “Substrate Dependence Score” yet

A number looks useful. An unvalidated number is worse than a precise finding.

MIRRORNODE will not compress substrate dependence into a score until the rubric, weighting, evidence classes, uncertainty handling, and repeatability are strong enough to justify it.

Until then, the audit says what the evidence supports and preserves what it cannot establish as unknown.

## Why measurement comes before remediation

The control-plane market is already full of strong products for gateways, routing, monitoring, policy, evaluations, runtime enforcement, and governance.

A customer may need one of them.

They may also need a simpler architecture, a different evidence practice, an external approval gate, a portable function contract, an owned inference path, or no new platform at all.

The audit should discover that before MIRRORNODE recommends infrastructure.

That is both better engineering and a cleaner commercial relationship.

## Where MIRRORNODE can go next

When an audit surfaces a real structural gap, a later engagement may address patterns such as:

- externalizing authority from model behavior;
- durable evidence and work records;
- capability acceptance suites;
- model/provider abstraction;
- owned inference;
- deterministic verification;
- controlled local operations;
- recovery and substitution drills.

But the audit does not manufacture those needs.

The evidence does.

## The proposition

MIRRORNODE is building systems in which the intelligence substrate can change without silently changing the organization's required function, authority boundary, or operating record.

Osiris is the first product because before you can build toward that state, you need to know exactly where you are not there today.
